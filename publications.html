      Liang Guo*

      Cybertissue neuroprosthetics: a new implantable neuroprosthetics paradigm to achieve seamless lifetime integration

      Journal of Neural Engineering, 23(5): 053003, 2026

      DOI

      传统电极式神经接口长期植入后，会因慢性异物反应和纤维包裹而信号衰减，这是植入式神经假体难以终身使用的根本障碍。本文提出“赛博组织神经假体”范式：把植入体本身设计成一种功能组织，以活细胞为主体，辅以极少量纳米电子、纳米材料或基因修饰，使其在结构和功能上与宿主神经系统融为一体，从而实现长期稳定的神经调控与信息交换。文章梳理了这一方向下的新兴研究，并讨论从工程化赛博细胞到可移植活体器件的机遇。Electrode-based neural interfaces lose signal over time to chronic foreign-body reactions and fibrotic encapsulation, a fundamental obstacle to lifelong implants. This perspective proposes cybertissue neuroprosthetics: designing the implant itself as a functional tissue made mainly of living cells, optionally with minimal nanoelectronics, nanomaterials or genetic modification, so that it integrates structurally and functionally with the host nervous system for stable long-term neuromodulation and information exchange. It brings together emerging work under this umbrella and discusses opportunities from engineered cybercells to transplantable living devices.

      关键词Keywords cybertissue · living neural implant · neural interface · neuromodulation · seamless integration · tissue engineering
