郭亮Liang Guo

特聘教授 · 上海交通大学集成电路学院（信息与电子工程学院）Distinguished Professor · School of Integrated Circuits (School of Information and Electronic Engineering), Shanghai Jiao Tong University

郭亮，上海交通大学特聘教授，从事脑机接口与神经接口工程研究二十余年。他本科毕业于清华大学生物医学工程专业，在佐治亚理工学院获得生物医学工程博士学位，随后在麻省理工学院 Robert S. Langer 实验室从事博士后研究。Liang Guo is a Distinguished Professor at Shanghai Jiao Tong University who has worked on brain–computer and neural interfaces for more than two decades. He received his B.S. in Biomedical Engineering from Tsinghua University and his Ph.D. in Biomedical Engineering from the Georgia Institute of Technology, then did postdoctoral research in Robert S. Langer's lab at MIT.

2013 年起，他任教于美国俄亥俄州立大学，为电气与计算机工程系和神经科学系联聘教授，2021 年晋升副教授，其间获得 DARPA 青年教师奖与 NSF CAREER 奖。此后他在硅谷任亚马逊 Lab126 高级传感器架构师，并创办神经科技公司 Twibetu, Inc.。2025 年，他全职回国加入上海交通大学。From 2013 he was on the faculty of The Ohio State University, jointly appointed in Electrical and Computer Engineering and in Neuroscience, and was promoted to Associate Professor in 2021. There he received the DARPA Young Faculty Award and the NSF CAREER Award. He then worked in Silicon Valley as a Senior Sensor Architect at Amazon Lab126 and founded the neurotechnology company Twibetu, Inc., before joining Shanghai Jiao Tong University full-time in 2025.

他的研究聚焦神经再生型脑机接口、微创与非侵入式高性能超声脑机接口，以及脑机融合理论。His research focuses on neuroregenerative brain–computer interfaces, minimally invasive and non-invasive high-performance ultrasound brain–computer interfaces, and the theory of brain–machine fusion.

办公室Office上海交通大学闵行校区，上海市闵行区东川路 800 号（房间号待补充）Minhang Campus, Shanghai Jiao Tong University, 800 Dongchuan Road, Shanghai (room to be added)
