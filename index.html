脑智融合实验室Lab English Name

脑智融合实验室致力于理解大脑如何处理信息，并将这些原理与人工智能方法相结合。我们以脑机接口为核心方向，研究如何从脑电、皮层脑电与神经影像信号中准确解码人的感知、意图与认知状态，进而构建安全、可靠、能够长期使用的人机交互系统。We study how the brain processes information and bring those principles together with artificial intelligence. Brain–computer interfaces are at the centre of our work: we develop methods that decode perception, intention and cognitive state from EEG, ECoG and neuroimaging signals, and we build human–machine interaction systems that are safe, reliable and fit for long-term use.

我们的研究横跨神经科学、信号处理与机器学习。一方面，我们发展高时空分辨率的神经信号采集与解码算法；另一方面，我们借鉴神经环路的结构与学习机制，设计更高效、泛化能力更强的类脑智能模型。实验室与临床团队保持紧密合作，推动研究成果走向神经康复与辅助交流等实际应用。Our research spans neuroscience, signal processing and machine learning. On one side, we develop recording and decoding methods with high spatial and temporal resolution; on the other, we draw on the structure and learning rules of neural circuits to design brain-inspired models that are more efficient and generalise better. Working closely with clinical partners, we aim to bring these advances into neurorehabilitation and assistive communication.

我们同样重视人才培养，鼓励学生在严谨的实验设计与开放的学术交流中成长。欢迎具有神经科学、计算机、电子工程、数学或心理学背景的同学加入我们。We care just as much about training people. We encourage students to grow through rigorous experimental design and open scientific exchange, and we welcome applicants with backgrounds in neuroscience, computer science, electrical engineering, mathematics or psychology.
