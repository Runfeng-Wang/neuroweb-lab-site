研究方向Research

实验室围绕三个相互支撑的方向开展研究：让植入体与神经组织长成一体，用超声读写大脑，并为大脑与机器的融合建立理论基础。Our work follows three directions that support one another: implants that grow into neural tissue, ultrasound that reads from and writes to the brain, and a theoretical basis for fusing brains with machines.

01

神经再生型脑机接口Neuroregenerative Brain–Computer Interfaces

传统电极植入大脑后会引发异物反应和纤维包裹，信号随时间衰减，难以长期使用。我们把植入体本身当作一种功能组织来设计：以活细胞为主体，只辅以极少量的纳米电子器件或基因修饰，让它在结构和功能上与宿主神经系统长成一体，实现终身稳定的神经调控与信息交换。我们称之为“赛博组织神经假体”。Conventional electrodes trigger foreign-body reactions and fibrotic encapsulation, so their signals fade over time. We design the implant itself as a functional tissue — built mainly from living cells, with only minimal nanoelectronics or genetic modification — so that it integrates structurally and functionally with the host nervous system and supports stable neuromodulation and information exchange for a lifetime. We call this approach cybertissue neuroprosthetics.

  • 活体神经植入Living neural implants
  • 组织工程Tissue engineering
  • 长期稳定集成Lifetime integration

02

超声脑机接口Ultrasound Brain–Computer Interfaces

功能超声成像能以高时空分辨率读取大范围脑区的血流动力学活动，而且只需微创甚至无需开颅。我们发展微创与非侵入式的高性能超声脑机接口，包括超声成像硬件、实时意图解码算法，以及利用超声敏感离子通道调控神经元的“声遗传学”方法，让超声既能读、也能写。Functional ultrasound imaging reads haemodynamic activity across large brain regions with high spatial and temporal resolution, through minimally invasive or even non-invasive access. We build high-performance ultrasound brain–computer interfaces: imaging hardware, real-time intention decoding, and acoustogenetics, which controls neurons through ultrasound-sensitive ion channels — so ultrasound can both read and write.

  • 功能超声成像Functional ultrasound
  • 实时解码Real-time decoding
  • 声遗传学Acoustogenetics

03

脑机融合理论Theory of Brain–Machine Fusion

好的神经接口需要好的理论。我们从生物物理出发，研究电记录与电刺激在神经组织中的作用原理，建立描述大脑与人工系统之间信息交换的理论框架，并用它指导下一代神经接口与类脑智能系统的设计。Good neural interfaces need good theory. Starting from biophysics, we study how electrical recording and stimulation act in neural tissue, build a theoretical framework for information exchange between brains and artificial systems, and use it to guide the design of next-generation neural interfaces and brain-inspired intelligence.

  • 神经接口生物物理Neural-interface biophysics
  • 信息交换Information exchange
  • 类脑智能Brain-inspired AI
